Why abstain?

When a required extractor, origin, or observation is unavailable, the monitor must not infer a successful handoff.

Where the boundary ends

The tests exercise local parsers, contracts, identity/version checks, DAG boundaries, and limited declared retries. They do not certify authentic remote custody.

How to interpret the evidence

01 · Contract-aware

Compares declared sender and receiver states against required artifacts, versions, rules and allowed handoff structure.

02 · Byte-grounded

Uses actual local bytes for supported checks. A matching hash does not authenticate who produced or received the file.

03 · Refuse unsupported claims

When evidence is missing or unsupported, the result remains UNKNOWN rather than assuming success. Lot 5's separate trust boundary is not closed.
